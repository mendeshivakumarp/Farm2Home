Our Mission

Connecting Local Farmers Directly With Your Table

Farm2Home was founded with a clear mission: to build a direct bridge between smallholder organic farmers and urban households. Traditional agricultural supply chains force farmers to lose up to 60% of their earnings to intermediaries while consumers pay higher prices for stale produce.

With Farm2Home, crops ordered in the morning are harvested fresh at sunrise and delivered straight to your home within hours, guaranteeing maximum flavor, nutrition, and fair revenue for hardworking farming families.
